Who We Are

A Student-Led Consulting Organization.

180 Degrees Consulting President University is a student-led consulting organization focused on developing student capabilities while exploring solutions to real-world organizational and business challenges.


We encourage students to learn through collaboration, structured problem-solving, research, and practical consulting experiences.

Our Vision

To develop purpose-driven student consultants who create meaningful and sustainable impact through strategic thinking and collaboration.

Our Mission

01. Develop Capabilities

Develop student consulting, analytical, communication, and leadership skills.

02. Deliver Solutions

Deliver structured and practical solutions to organizational challenges.

03. Build Relationships

Build meaningful relationships with organizations, communities, and academic stakeholders.

04. Encourage Innovation

Encourage innovation, collaboration, continuous learning, and sustainable impact.

SERVICE 01

Business Strategy & Growth

Focus: Business direction and growth opportunities.


Scope: Strategic assessment, opportunity mapping, business development, and growth planning.


Potential deliverables: Strategic recommendations, opportunity analysis, and growth roadmap.

SERVICE 02

Market Research & Customer Analysis

Focus: Market understanding and customer insights.


Scope: Market trends, customer segmentation, competitor research, and customer needs.


Potential deliverables: Market research report, customer profiles, and competitor analysis.

SERVICE 03

Financial Analysis & Business Planning

Focus: Financial understanding and business planning.


Scope: Financial performance review, budgeting concepts, and business feasibility analysis.


Potential deliverables: Financial analysis, business plan, and planning recommendations.

SERVICE 04

Marketing & Branding Strategy

Focus: Market positioning and communication.


Scope: Brand positioning, marketing channels, communication strategy, and campaign planning.


Potential deliverables: Marketing strategy, brand assessment, and campaign recommendations.

SERVICE 05

Operational Improvement

Focus: Operational processes and efficiency.


Scope: Workflow assessment, process mapping, and operational improvement opportunities.


Potential deliverables: Process analysis, workflow recommendations, and improvement roadmap.

SERVICE 06

Business Model & Digital Development

Focus: Business model and digital opportunities.


Scope: Business model assessment, value proposition, and digital development opportunities.


Potential deliverables: Business model canvas, digital opportunity assessment, and recommendations.

How We Work

A Clear Process From Start to Finish.

01. Understand

Understand the organization, objectives, challenges, and expectations.

02. Investigate

Collect relevant information and conduct structured research and analysis.

03. Recommend

Develop recommendations and communicate findings clearly.

The scope and deliverables of each engagement depend on the agreed objectives and available resources. Our student consulting activities do not replace licensed audit, tax, legal, or other regulated professional services.
